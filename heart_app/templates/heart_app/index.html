📝 About the Features

  • Chest Pain Type: ASY (Asymptomatic) is the strongest predictor of disease.
  • Oldpeak: ST segment depression induced by exercise relative to rest.
  • ST Slope: Slope of peak exercise ST segment — Flat/Down slope is concerning.
  • Fasting BS: Blood sugar above 120 mg/dl may indicate diabetes, a risk factor.
  • Exercise Angina: Chest pain during exercise strongly indicates disease.
  • Max HR: Lower maximum heart rate may correlate with higher risk.
